ACADEMIC VISIT

By Jean-Marc Tallon November 24, 2026

Prof. Yangsiyu Lu invited Prof. Jean-Marc Tallon, Research Professor at the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS) and Professor at the Paris School of Economics, to visit HKUST(GZ) through the university’s Cross-disciplinary Research Funding for Visiting Scholars program (CRFVS).

During his visit from 16 to 24 November, Prof. Tallon participated in a series of teaching, research, and exchange activities. He joined the group meeting of the Energy Infrastructure and Climate Policy (EICP) Lab, delivered a guest lecture for the CNCC 5600 Energy Economics and Policy course, and took part in an afternoon joint seminar hosted by CNCC and IPE. He also held meetings with faculty, students, and the Global Affairs department.

This visit promoted international academic exchange and created opportunities for future interdisciplinary research collaboration between HKUST(GZ), Prof. Jean-Marc Tallon, and related research communities.
